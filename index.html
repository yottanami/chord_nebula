How to Play

Connect a MIDI keyboard (or a MIDI-capable app on your phone), then pick your key and level from the setup screen.

Notes and chords fall from the top of the play area. Play the note or chord shown — all its notes at once, on your MIDI keyboard — before it reaches the bottom.

Match it in time and it pops; let it fall past the bottom and you lose a life. Run out of lives and the game ends.

Levels 1-3 are free and cover scale notes and basic chords. Levels 4-8 add note ordering, root-in-bass voicings, seventh chords, and more — unlock them with a one-time purchase from the setup screen.

About

Chord Nebula teaches the keyboard in the order the music itself is built. You start with the notes of a key, one at a time, until the shape of the scale is under your fingers.

Then come the chords built on those notes: how each one is stacked, what it is called, and which degree of the key it belongs to.

The part that matters most comes last. Chords are rarely played alone, so the game drills the common progressions those chords make in real music, across keys, scales, and genres, until moving between them stops being a calculation and becomes something you hear.
